Step 1

Remove the old icon

Press and hold the old TUF icon on your Home Screen, then choose Remove App (iPhone) or Uninstall (Android).

Step 2

Open the new link in your browser

Tap Open the new app above. Use Safari on an iPhone or Chrome on Android. If you opened this page from WhatsApp, Teams or email, open the link in your browser first, or the install option won’t appear. If it asks you to sign in, use your school Google account.

Step 3

Add it to your Home Screen

  1. iPhone: tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: tap the three dots, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
